# RainFlix 1.0.0 for Android

RainFlix on Android pairs the shared catalog and personal library with native,
immersive playback and mobile-first controls.

## Highlights

- Catalog grids render on scroll, with add-on catalog lookups deferred until their rows enter view.
- Dark-backed R launcher icon matching the startup loader, including adaptive Android icons.

- Native Media3 playback and app-owned torrent support without a separate Stremio player.
- Landscape immersive playback around status bars and display cutouts.
- Center and bottom play/pause and seek controls, double-tap seeking, and hardware volume support.
- Language-grouped audio/subtitles and subtitle appearance controls.
- Scrollable episode/provider/source selection with thumbnails, provider logos, and readable stream details.
- Episode navigation, automatic next, optional intro/outro skipping, next-episode preview, and best-effort matching-source prefetch.
- Persistent installed add-ons, My List, Continue Watching, and Watched; long-press opens history actions.
- Compact three-column mobile cards and infinite-scroll search/Browse.
- More like this stays hidden while selecting streams so the selection flow remains focused.
- Default reduced-animation mode with a solid charcoal-gray background.
- R startup artwork instead of a brief plain-text launch screen.
- Weekly cached startup posters with bundled first-install artwork and smoother background transitions when animations are enabled.
- One horizontal buffering indicator and consolidated add-on no-streams messages.

## Installation

Install the signed ARM64 APK (`app-arm64-release.apk`, optionally distributed as
`RainFlix_1.0.0_android-arm64.apk`). Approve installation from your download
source when Android asks. Keep the existing app and data when updating; the
release must be signed with the same RainFlix key.

## Notes

This APK targets ARM64 devices. Video decoding depends on the device and is
not identical to Windows mpv. Unavailable peers, DRM, unsupported codecs, or
provider failures may prevent playback. Casting and background torrent
playback are not included. Android launchers may apply their own icon mask.
